RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This agreement is for an 11 months term.

Monthly Rent: ₹20,000 per month.
Security Deposit: ₹60,000.
Lock-in Period: 6 months.
Notice Period: 2 months.

The tenant shall be responsible for routine maintenance and minor repairs.
Electricity and water utilities shall be paid by the tenant.

The tenant shall provide written notice of not less than 2 months prior to vacating the premises.
The security deposit shall be refunded subject to the conditions stated in this agreement.
Late payment may attract a penalty.
